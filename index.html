Our Focus

Five things that guide
everything we do.

These convictions shape how we serve communities, support local pastors, and follow where God leads to make Christ known.

01

Reaching Those Who Don't Know Christ

We carry a deep burden for those who do not yet know Christ, especially those in remote and underserved communities. Every person matters, and every opportunity to make Christ known matters.

02

Strengthening Pastors & Churches

Faithful local pastors and churches are essential for lasting ministry. We walk alongside them by equipping, encouraging, and resourcing their work in the communities they serve.

03

Serving Through Practical Love

We meet people where they are through medical camps, community service, and tangible acts of love that reflect the compassion and generosity of Christ.

04

Discernment & Prayer

Every step is taken through prayer and Scripture. We seek to move where God leads, aligning our ways with His Word and will rather than our own plans or timelines.

05

His Ministry. Our Stewardship.

This is not our ministry — it is His. We are stewards of what God has entrusted to us, serving with faithfulness and humility until He returns.

What We Believe

Rooted in the
Word of God.

This mission is built on biblical convictions that keep Scripture, salvation, the church, and holy living at the center.

Scripture

The Bible is the inspired Word of God and our final authority for faith and life.

The Trinity

There is one God in three Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, equal in nature and glory, distinct in Person.

Salvation

Salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone, not by works, but through His finished work.

The Church

The local church is God's ordained instrument for worship, discipleship, fellowship, and mission.

Baptism

Believer's baptism by immersion is a public declaration of faith and identification with Christ.

The Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit indwells, empowers, and guides every believer, producing spiritual fruit and giving gifts for the building up of the body of Christ.

Holy Living

We are called to live set apart, pursuing holiness as a response to God's grace, not as a means of earning it.

Eschatology

Christ will return bodily, visibly, and gloriously. We live with urgency and hope in that promise.

Family & Conscience

We affirm God's design for the family and honor individual conscience as it is informed and governed by Scripture.
