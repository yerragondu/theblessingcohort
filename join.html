Carried together.

Right now, this work is carried by a small group of people within our circle who felt led to pray, encourage, and support what God is doing.

As the work continues to grow, we're opening the door for others to learn about the mission, pray, and discern whether God may be leading them to carry a part of it too.

How the work is structured

Find where God is at work. Join Him there.

We seek to stay close to the field, make decisions prayerfully, and respond faithfully to where God is leading.

  1. Discernment through relationship and prayer

    We look for where God is already at work through faithful pastors and local churches. Before entering into partnership, we spend time in the field, build relationships, observe the work firsthand, and prayerfully discern where He is leading.

  2. Partnership through encouragement and support

    When there is alignment, we come alongside pastors and churches with encouragement, ongoing practical and financial support, helping ensure they are not serving alone.

  3. Serving communities through local churches

    Working alongside local churches, we seek to meet practical and spiritual needs within their communities, demonstrating the love of Christ through service, care, and Gospel witness.

  4. Walking together with accountability

    We stay close to the work through regular visits, relationships, and ongoing discernment. Rather than leading from a distance, we walk alongside those serving in the field and steward the work faithfully.

Clarity matters.

We want you to understand how the work is carried before deciding whether to be part of it.

What happens to the support entrusted to the mission?

Every contribution is directed toward the work in the field. Administrative, transportation, and operational expenses are personally covered by us as a commitment before God.

Can I stay connected before making a decision?

Absolutely. You are welcome to ask questions, follow the work, and take time to pray before deciding whether and how to get involved.

Why do you ask people to pray before getting involved?

You may have found this mission through someone you know, or simply through this website. Either way, we don't expect you to decide based only on what you see or what we say. Take it to God in prayer — ask for wisdom and discernment, and seek His leading before deciding whether to walk alongside this work.

You've seen the work. You've seen how we work. Now: pray, discern, ask your questions, and respond as God leads.
